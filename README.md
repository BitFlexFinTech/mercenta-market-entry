# Mercenta Market Entry

Build FOUR COMPLETE, DISTINCTIVE HOMEPAGE DESIGNS for Mercenta Partners Ltd in ONE Lovable project, in ONE generation pass.

This is strictly a frontend design showcase. Do not build backend, DB, auth, forms, APIs, or interactive app logic. Visual design and composition only.

# SPECIAL REQUIREMENT — TOP-RIGHT ANIMATION FROM DEMO.MERCENTA.CO.UK:
We must preserve and integrate the live Shipment Tracker animation from the current demo site (demo.mercenta.co.uk) into the top-right of the hero section (especially in Concept 3 — Modern UK Market-Entry Platform, and available across the designs):
- Top header: "SHIPMENT TRACKER" in uppercase monospace tracking-[0.25em] with a live pulsing green beacon (ping animation: `animate-tracker-ping` scaling and fading) and "EXAMPLE" badge.
- Animated route line: Dotted transit line with waypoints (Origin, In Transit, UK Arrival in orange) and an animated travelling glowing orange dot (`animate-route-dot` 6s linear infinite, w-3.5 h-3.5 with orange box-shadow 0 0 12px #FF6A00).
- Live shipment stage card: Reference code (e.g., "MCT-2418 · Consumer goods"), freight badge ("AIR FREIGHT"), and sequential milestone stages with checkmarks and an active "IN PROGRESS" orange pill badge ("Origin collected", "Export airport", "Air transit", "UK customs cleared", "On shelf").
- Subtitle: "An example of the live view partner brands see once onboarded".

# 1. ABOUT MERCENTA & POSITIONING
Mercenta Partners Ltd is a UK-focused wholesale distribution and market-entry platform helping international brands enter, establish and grow in the UK market.
Core capabilities:
- UK market-entry infrastructure
- Wholesale distribution
- Shipping and storage coordination
- Retail-channel operations
- Brand growth and commercial development
- Technology-enabled distribution infrastructure

Approved tagline:
**Global Brands. UK Distribution. Smarter Growth.**

Target audiences:
- International brands seeking UK market entry
- Established businesses looking for UK distribution partners
- Commercial partners
- Institutional investors and funders
- Strategic business partners

CRITICAL RESTRICTION: Do not publicly mention Amazon Vendor Central, Amazon 1P or reveal the underlying sales-channel relationship. Do not invent client testimonials, partnerships, client logos, financial figures, warehouse capacities, certifications or performance claims.

# 2. BRAND PALETTE & IDENTITY
Strict palette:
- Black: #000000
- White: #FFFFFF
- Orange: #FF6A00
Orange must be a meaningful part of the visual identity, not merely a tiny button accent. Confident use of black, white and orange.
Strictly forbidden: No purple, no teal, no generic AI-style gradients, no generic startup aesthetics, no generic luxury-fashion typography, no excessive rounded cards.
Logo: Refined text-based MERCENTA wordmark with a distinctive lowercase 'm' mark.

# 3. FOUR DISTINCT HOMEPAGE CONCEPTS
Build all four concepts completely with desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts:

1. `/concept-1` — THE BOLD ORANGE WORLD
- Strong visual impact, dominant orange sections and backgrounds.
- Confident black and white typography, large memorable headlines, strong geometric composition.
- Established international brand advertising identity feel.

2. `/concept-2` — THE PREMIUM DISTRIBUTION PARTNER
- Sophisticated corporate foundation in black and white with strategic, confident orange accents.
- High commercial credibility for institutional partners and investors.
- Refined structured content, commercial logistics photography, crisp grid.

3. `/concept-3` — THE MODERN UK MARKET-ENTRY PLATFORM
- Contemporary technology-enabled platform aesthetic.
- Prominently features the Shipment Tracker route animation from demo.mercenta.co.uk in the top-right of the hero!
- Clean layouts, strong information hierarchy, interface-inspired diagrams and visual representations of distribution infrastructure.
- Confident balance between technology and physical distribution.

4. `/concept-4` — THE DISTINCTIVE EDITORIAL BRAND
- Strong editorial art direction, oversized typography, asymmetrical sections, confident use of whitespace.
- Striking orange, black, and white visual blocks with high editorial polish.

# 4. REQUIRED CONTENT SECTIONS (Each concept must have all sections complete):
- Header / Navigation (visual wordmark, links, CTA)
- Hero section (tagline, bold headline, supporting copy, high-impact imagery / interactive visual)
- Distribution infrastructure (Wholesale distribution, Shipping & logistics, Storage & fulfilment, Retail-channel operations, Commercial growth)
- How it works (Visually engaging step-by-step)
- Why Mercenta (UK market knowledge, infrastructure, coordination, tech-enabled, long-term growth)
- Who we work with (Categories: Home, Office supplies, Kitchenware, Health & personal care, Baby products, Consumer electronics, Automotive accessories, Groceries, Apparel, Beer & wine)
- Final Call to Action (Commercial partnership invitation)
- Footer

# 5. NAVIGATION & SHOWCASE
- Root route `/` is a clean, minimal Concept Selection / Comparison Hub introducing all 4 concepts with summaries and direct links.
- Every concept page includes a persistent, unobtrusive floating switcher bar allowing instant jumping between Concept 1, 2, 3, 4, or back to the Hub `/`.
- Responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Use realistic, high-quality commercial distribution and logistics imagery.
